Banking Analytics: Geospatial Customer Demand & Branch Performance

Built an end-to-end geospatial analytics solution using Python, SQL, and Tableau to identify underserved banking regions and forecast customer demand. Analyzed 150 branches across Canada, identifying $2.5M in monthly transaction gaps and recommending data-driven expansion strategies.

Retail Sales & Inventory Analysis - Northwind Traders

Analyzed 11+ years of sales and inventory data using advanced SQL to uncover seasonal trends, identify high-value products, and detect critical stockouts. Delivered actionable insights for inventory optimization, revenue protection, and operational efficiency, showcasing skills in data analysis, business intelligence, and strategic reporting.

Toronto Bike Share Ridership Analysis

Analyzed Toronto Bike Share data to uncover daily and hourly usage trends, popular stations, and weekday vs weekend patterns using Python (pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy). Generated insights presented in a comprehensive report.

Predicting Home Prices

Developed a Python-based linear regression model using scikit-learn to predict house prices from square footage. Visualized results with Matplotlib and explored data with Pandas and NumPy.

Student Performance Analysis

Cleaned and analyzed student survey data to explore how attendance, homework, and teacher–parent communication relate to exam scores. Created interactive dashboards in Tableau and Power BI, and summarized trends with Excel PivotTables, gaining hands-on experience in data cleaning, analysis, and visualization across multiple platforms.

Nutrovision

Worked in a team to develop NutroVision, an AI-powered recipe and nutrition app that identifies fruits and vegetables from images, suggests personalized recipes, and provides detailed calorie and nutritional visualizations. The project won the Best Health & Wellness Hack award at hackHer 2024.
